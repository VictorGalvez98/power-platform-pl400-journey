This section contains the technical knowledge and concepts I learn throughout my Power Platform PL-400 journey.

The objective is not simply to collect notes, but to understand concepts well enough to apply and explain them in practical scenarios.

##  Learning Approach

For each important topic, I aim to understand:

1. What it is
2. Why it is useful
3. How it works
4. When to use it
5. Limitations and common problems
6. How I applied it
7. What I learned

##  Employability Focus

The goal is to develop knowledge that can be demonstrated through practical evidence and discussed during technical interviews.

Learning is therefore connected to:

- Practical exercises
- Experiments
- Troubleshooting
- Technical decisions
- The practical project
